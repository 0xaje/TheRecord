# AI-assisted work and human verification

TheRecord was developed with AI assistance for planning, code implementation, tests, troubleshooting, documentation and visual direction. The founder selected the product and policies, approved material changes, operated the wallets and supplied phone-testing results. Do not describe the work as unassisted or the generated audit reports as independent human certification.

AI-assisted areas include browser/Worker code, agreement integration and scripts, isolated failure/migration tests, research synthesis and README. Primary research links and interpretations are recorded in docs/research/WHY_THE_RECORD.md. The finding-response ledgers preserve unresolved/disputed items.

Tests using RPC/precompile/failure fixtures are separated from genuine Diana receipts and historical calls. The founder operated both test wallets; independent-user acceptance remains pending. Passing tests do not establish universal correctness.
